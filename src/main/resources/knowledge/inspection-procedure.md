# Sector inspection procedure

Confirm the selected sector and obtain a route estimate before inspecting it.
Account for three additional battery percentage points beyond movement energy.
The simulator's completed inspection records whether an anomaly was found and
a description of that finding. An inspection of SECTOR_C reports a vehicle;
the other initial sectors have no simulated anomaly.

After execution, read the mission's recorded inspection result and distinguish
observed findings from assumptions. A planned inspection or a route quote is not
an observation. No camera image is analyzed in this milestone; multimodal image
analysis is a later feature. Report unusual findings without inventing evidence.
